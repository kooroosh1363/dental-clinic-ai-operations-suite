# Roadmap

## Version 1.1 — operational depth

- Calendar rescheduling and conflict detection
- Patient detail timeline and consent history
- Notification-template administration
- Knowledge article review/version UI
- CSV export with permission and audit controls

## Version 1.2 — integration readiness

- Adapter interface for dental practice-management systems
- Provider-backed email/SMS with delivery webhooks
- OpenTelemetry traces, metrics and operational alerts
- SSO, MFA and server-side session revocation

## Version 2 — evaluated intelligence

- Multilingual retrieval with a versioned evaluation corpus
- Staff feedback and answer-quality review workflow
- Calibrated no-show model trained only after fairness, privacy and drift review
- Clinic-specific policy packs and approval matrices

Items are proposals, not promises. Clinical diagnosis, autonomous treatment selection and unsupervised patient messaging are intentionally out of scope.
